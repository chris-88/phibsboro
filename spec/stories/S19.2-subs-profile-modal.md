|---|---|

## Goal
A member sees their own subs on their profile with a Pay button (S19.2), and a member who still owes
gets a dismissible "you owe €X — Pay" reminder on app open (S19.3, the auto half of Y4).

## Scope
**In**
- `useMySubs` — the caller's own standing (club amount − their own payments) + the pay link.
- **Profile subs card**: outstanding / paid, and a Pay button to the admin-set link (or "paid up").
  Hidden while loading, on error, or when subs aren't set up (amount 0).
- **Reminder modal**: mounted at the root; shows for a signed-in member with an outstanding balance,
  once per session (dismissible → re-appears on the next open until paid). "Pay now" / "Later".

**Out**
- The manager team view + the manager WhatsApp nudge (S19.4). Recording payments (admin, S19.1).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **AC1** — The profile shows the member's outstanding / paid and a Pay link.
- [x] **AC2** — A member who owes sees the reminder on app open; paid-up members don't.
- [x] **AC3** — "Later" dismisses for the session; it returns on the next open until paid.
- [x] **AC4** — Both are read-only for the member and hide entirely when subs aren't configured.

## Implementation
- `src/api/subs.ts` — `useMySubs` (composes settings + own payments).
- `src/features/subs/components/SubsCard.tsx` (in `ProfileScreen`) and `SubsReminderModal.tsx`
  (mounted in `RootLayout` for a signed-in user).

## The report
#4 "Let's capture injury status of players where they can be marked as injured themselves or by a manager
and provide updates on date of return etc"

## Goal
A player can be marked **injured** — by themselves or a manager of their team — with an **expected return
date** and a short note, visible where the squad is managed.

## Proposed shape (Z2, confirm)
- Data: an injury on the player — `status` (fit / injured), `expected_return` (date, nullable), `note`,
  `updated_by`, `updated_at`. A single current record per player (not a history) unless Chris wants history.
- Write: the player themselves **or** a manager of a team they're on (security-definer RPC, audited).
- Surface: an **Injured** flag + return date on the profile, the member list and the squad picker; optional
  — an injured player is skipped from the availability "awaiting" chase.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **AC1** — A player marks themselves injured with an expected return; a manager can too, for their team.
- [ ] **AC2** — The flag + return date show on the roster / squad picker / profile.
- [ ] **AC3** — RLS: only self or a team manager writes it; everyone on the team reads it.
- [ ] **AC4** — Four states.

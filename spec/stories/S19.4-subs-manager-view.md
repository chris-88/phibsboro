## Goal
A manager sees who on their team has paid and who's behind, and can nudge a player who still owes over
WhatsApp (the manager half of Y4). Completes Epic 19.

## Scope
**In**
- A **Subs** segment on the Squad tab (Selection · Game Stats · Members · Subs): the team's members,
  most-owing first, each with a paid-up / "€X left" badge.
- A **Remind** per player still owing → a WhatsApp message (`buildSubsReminderMessage`) with the
  amount and the pay link.
- Read-only: recording payments stays the admin's job (S19.1); the manager sees the RLS-scoped rows.

**Out**
- Recording / undoing payments (admin, S19.1). Editing the amount (admin, S19.1).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **AC1** — The Squad tab's Subs view lists the team's members with paid/outstanding, most-owing first.
- [x] **AC2** — A "Remind" opens a WhatsApp message with the amount + pay link, for unpaid players only.
- [x] **AC3** — Four states; a clear empty when subs aren't set up.
- [x] **AC4** — The manager sees only their team's rows (RLS); they cannot record payments.

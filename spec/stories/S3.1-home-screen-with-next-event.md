## Goal

A player opens the app and the first thing on the screen is the next thing they have to turn up to, with
YES and NO under it. One tap answers it. Coming back later, they can see at a glance what they said. A
player with nothing on sees a plain line saying so, not a blank screen.

## Context

`CLAUDE.md` asks for a "Prominent next-event card: type badge, title, day and time, location, and large
YES / NO buttons", a one-tap optimistic response, an obvious current answer, and an empty state. The
decisions that shape it, from [00-decisions.md](../00-decisions.md):

- **D60** the card shows the next `status = 'scheduled'` event only. A cancelled event never occupies it;
  it stays in the S3.2 list below, marked cancelled.
- **D48** fixes the optimistic contract: `onMutate` snapshots, `onError` restores and shows "Couldn't save.
  Tap again." with the buttons still enabled, `onSettled` invalidates. No offline write queue. The
  after-`starts_at` state is judged against a server timestamp, never the device clock.
- **D61** a response cannot be cleared. Tapping the selected answer is a no-op and no deselect affordance is
  rendered.
- **D41** `/` sits inside the app shell, so the fixed bottom nav is present and page content is
  bottom-padded to clear it.
- **D22** a player may hold memberships on more than one team, and managers are ordinary squad members who
  also respond. The card is the next event across **all** the player's teams, not one team's.
- **D39** `event_responses(user_id)` is indexed, so the own-response read is cheap.
- **D40** the shadcn `Button` size variants are already overridden to 44px minimum in S0.2.

This story is built after [S3.3](S3.3-event-detail-screen.md) and
[S3.4](S3.4-changing-a-response.md) (build order D20, position 25). It therefore **consumes** rather than
creates: `AvailabilityButtons`, `useSetResponse` and `serverNow()` all already exist. What is
new here is the home route, the card, and the `useUpcomingEvents` query that
[S3.2](S3.2-upcoming-events-list.md) will also read.

## Scope

**In**
- Route `/`, inside the app shell, guarded by S2.9.
- `useUpcomingEvents()` in `src/api/events.ts` — all upcoming events across the player's teams, with the
  player's own response on each, in one round trip.
- `NextEventCard`: type badge, title, day and time, location, team name when the player has more than one
  team, and the shared availability buttons.
- Current answer rendered unmistakably on return.
- Two distinct empty states: no team memberships, and no upcoming scheduled event.
- Loading skeleton, inline error with Retry.

**Out**
- The list below the card — [S3.2](S3.2-upcoming-events-list.md). This story reserves the slot and ships
  the shared query; it renders nothing there.
- The availability buttons themselves, the mutation, the rollback copy and the server clock — all
  [S3.3](S3.3-event-detail-screen.md).
- The disabled-after-`starts_at` state and its explanatory line — [S3.4](S3.4-changing-a-response.md).
- The install and escape prompts that fire after a first response — [S2.7](S2.7-escape-the-whatsapp-browser.md),
  [S2.8](S2.8-add-to-home-screen-guide.md). This story leaves a single documented mount point and nothing
  else.
- Past events and attendance — [S3.5](S3.5-own-attendance-history.md).
- Anything manager-facing: counts, response lists, create buttons. Epic 4.
- Relative day labels ("Tonight", "Tomorrow"). No helper exists, D35 fixes the three formats, and inventing
  a fourth would put a second date formatter in the codebase.
- A weather line, a map, a calendar button, a squad list, a "12 going" figure. Not in the brief, and D32
  makes the last one impossible for a player anyway.

## Acceptance criteria

Each criterion is independently checkable by a person or a test.

- [ ] **AC1** — Signed in with at least one upcoming scheduled event, `/` renders exactly one next-event
      card, for the event with the smallest `starts_at` greater than `serverNow()` across every team the
      player belongs to.
- [ ] **AC2** — The card shows: a type badge reading "Match" or "Training", the title,
      `formatEventTime(starts_at, 'share')`, and the location. No other date formatter is used anywhere in
      the file.
- [ ] **AC3** — The team name is shown on the card when the player belongs to more than one team, and is
      omitted when they belong to exactly one.
- [ ] **AC4** — A cancelled event never appears in the card, even when it is the soonest upcoming event
      (D60). The card shows the next scheduled event instead, or the empty state if there is none.
- [ ] **AC5** — An event whose `starts_at` is in the past never appears in the card, judged against
      `serverNow()`.
- [ ] **AC6** — Events belonging to a team the player is no longer a member of do not appear, even when the
      player still holds a response row for them (D33 keeps the row readable; this screen is about teams
      you are on).
- [ ] **AC7** — Tapping YES or NO updates the card before the request resolves, and the answer survives a
      full page reload.
- [ ] **AC8** — On return, the chosen button is filled, the other outlined, the chosen button carries
      `aria-pressed="true"`, and a line under the buttons reads "You said yes." or "You said no." An
      unanswered event shows neither line.
- [ ] **AC9** — Tapping the already-selected answer fires no network request (D61).
- [ ] **AC10** — A failed write restores the previous state, leaves both buttons enabled, and shows
      "Couldn't save. Tap again." inline (D48).
- [ ] **AC11** — With no upcoming scheduled event but at least one team membership, the screen shows
      "Nothing coming up." and a second line, "Your manager will post the next one here." No card frame, no
      spinner, no error styling.
- [ ] **AC12** — With no team memberships at all, the screen shows "You're not on a team yet." and
      "Ask your manager for a join link." The bottom nav still renders.
- [ ] **AC13** — While loading, a skeleton of the same height as the populated card is shown. Nothing on the
      screen moves vertically when the data arrives.
- [ ] **AC14** — A failed load shows "Couldn't load your events." with a Retry control that refetches. The
      bottom nav stays usable.
- [ ] **AC15** — At 375x667 the card's YES and NO buttons are fully visible without scrolling, each at least
      44px tall, and clear of the fixed bottom nav.
- [ ] **AC16** — Returning to the app after it has been backgrounded refetches, so the card is not stale.
      `refetchOnWindowFocus` is on and `staleTime` is 30 seconds.
- [ ] **AC17** — Tapping the card anywhere above the buttons navigates to `/#/event/{id}`. Tapping YES or
      NO records the answer and does **not** navigate.

### The query

One read serves both this story and S3.2, keyed on the signed-in user so a sign-out clears it.

```ts
// src/api/events.ts
export type UpcomingEvent = {
  id: string
  teamId: string
  teamName: string
  type: Enums<'event_type'>
  title: string
  location: string
  startsAt: string
  status: Enums<'event_status'>
  myResponse: Enums<'availability_response'> | null
}

export function useUpcomingEvents(): UseQueryResult<UpcomingEvent[], PostgrestError>
```

```ts
const { id: userId, memberships } = useSignedInUser()   // S2.9 — valid below RequireAuth; throws elsewhere
const teamIds = memberships.map((m) => m.teamId)

useQuery({
  queryKey: eventKeys.upcoming(userId),
  enabled: teamIds.length > 0,
  staleTime: 30_000,
  refetchOnWindowFocus: true,
  queryFn: async () => {
    const { data, error } = await supabase
      .from('events')
      .select('id, team_id, type, title, location, starts_at, status, teams!inner(name), event_responses(response, user_id)')
      .in('team_id', teamIds)
      .gte('starts_at', serverNow().toISOString())
      .eq('event_responses.user_id', userId)
      .order('starts_at', { ascending: true })
      .order('id', { ascending: true })
      .limit(50)
    if (error) throw error
    return z.array(upcomingEventRowSchema).parse(data).map(toUpcomingEvent)
  },
})
```

Four things in that query are load-bearing:

- **`.in('team_id', teamIds)`** — RLS would already exclude other teams' events, but D33 also lets a player
  select any event they hold a response or attendance row for. Without this filter a player who left a team
  would still see that team's future fixtures. AC6.
- **`.eq('event_responses.user_id', user.id)`** — the same gotcha as S3.3. A manager's select policy returns
  the whole squad's response rows through the embed, so without the filter `event_responses[0]` is a
  teammate's answer. Keep it, and keep `.max(1)` on the Zod array so its removal fails a test rather than
  shipping.
- **`.gte('starts_at', serverNow().toISOString())`** — server clock, not `new Date()` (D48).
- **second `.order('id')`** — two events at the same instant would otherwise come back in an arbitrary
  order and the card would flip between refetches.

`enabled: teamIds.length > 0` means the no-memberships case never fires a request; `HomeScreen` reads
`memberships.length === 0` directly for AC12 rather than inferring it from an empty result.

`limit(50)` is a guard, not pagination. A squad does not have fifty future fixtures; if it ever does, the
tail is off the bottom of the S3.2 list and nobody has complained.

### Deriving the card

```ts
// pure, colocated with NextEventCard and unit tested without a database
export function pickNextEvent(events: readonly UpcomingEvent[]): UpcomingEvent | null {
  return events.find((e) => e.status === 'scheduled') ?? null
}
```

The query is already ordered ascending and already filtered to `starts_at > serverNow()`, so the first
scheduled row is the answer (D60). S3.2 renders `events.filter((e) => e.id !== next?.id)`, so the two
stories never disagree about which event is in the card.

### Screen structure

```tsx
<AppShell>                               {/* S0.2, bottom nav, pb for safe-area */}
  <h1 className="sr-only">Home</h1>
  {state === 'loading'   && <NextEventCardSkeleton />}
  {state === 'error'     && <ErrorState title="Couldn't load your events." onRetry={query.refetch} />}
  {state === 'noTeam'    && <EmptyState title="You're not on a team yet."
                                        body="Ask your manager for a join link." />}
  {state === 'noEvents'  && <EmptyState title="Nothing coming up."
                                        body="Your manager will post the next one here." />}
  {state === 'ready'     && <NextEventCard event={next} showTeamName={memberships.length > 1} />}
  {/* S3.2 mounts <UpcomingEventsList /> here */}
</AppShell>
```

Compute `state` once as a discriminated value rather than chaining ternaries, so S7.1's audit can read the
five branches off one switch. `noTeam` is checked before `loading`, because with no memberships the query
never runs and would otherwise sit in `isPending` for ever.

### Card layout

`Card` from shadcn. Order top to bottom: team name (small, muted, conditional), type badge, title (largest
text on the screen), date line, location, then the buttons. Buttons last so they land under the thumb.
`grid grid-cols-2 gap-3`, `min-h-14` each — AC15 is checked with a Playwright bounding-box assertion at
375x667, not by eye.

The card is a link target as well: tapping anywhere outside the buttons navigates to `/event/{id}`. Use a
`<Link>` wrapping the upper block only — never a `<Link>` wrapping the buttons, or a YES tap navigates
instead of answering.

### Response state, and the one thing not to build

`AvailabilityButtons` already owns the optimistic write, the rollback and the failure line (S3.3, D48).
This story passes `current={event.myResponse}` and nothing else. Do not add a second mutation, a second
snapshot, a toast, or a spinner on the buttons: a spinner on an optimistic control contradicts the point of
it.

`useSetResponse`'s `onMutate` writes into `eventKeys.upcoming(user.id)` as well as the detail key, so a tap
on the card and a tap on the detail screen keep the same cache honest.

### Hook for S2.7 and S2.8

Those stories need to know that a first response has just been submitted. Leave exactly one seam: after a
successful `useSetResponse`, `HomeScreen` renders `<PostResponsePrompts eventId={...} />` from
`src/features/events/components/PostResponsePrompts.tsx`, which ships in this story as a typed component
whose body is `return null`. No `localStorage` keys, no detection,
no copy. S2.7 and S2.8 fill it in and D46 decides which of the two shows. This is a mount point, not a TODO
for out-of-scope work.

## Data and API surface

**Tables read**
- `events` — filtered to the player's current team ids and to `starts_at >= serverNow()`.
- `teams` — embedded `teams!inner(name)`.
- `event_responses` — embedded, filtered to `user_id = auth.uid()`.

**Tables written**
- `event_responses`, through `useSetResponse` from S3.3. No new write path.

**RPCs called** — none. `team_member_directory` is not needed: this screen shows nobody else's name.

**Zod schemas added** (`src/features/events/schema.ts`)

```ts
export const upcomingEventRowSchema = eventRowSchema
  .pick({ id: true, team_id: true, type: true, title: true, location: true,
          starts_at: true, status: true })
  .extend({
    teams: z.object({ name: z.string() }),
    event_responses: z.array(z.object({ response: availabilityResponseSchema })).max(1),
  })
```

**Query keys** — `eventKeys.upcoming(userId)`, declared by S1.5 and first read by S3.3's mutation, first used as a query here. Invalidated by
`useSetResponse`'s `onSettled` via the `eventKeys.all` prefix.

**Mutations** — none new.

- **Unit** — Vitest. `pickNextEvent`: returns the first scheduled row; skips a cancelled row that sorts
  first (AC4); returns `null` for an all-cancelled list and for an empty list. Component tests with
  `@testing-library/react` and a `QueryClientProvider`, `useUpcomingEvents` and `useSignedInUser` stubbed:
  the five screen states each render their documented copy; the team name appears with two memberships and
  not with one (AC3); the answered card renders `aria-pressed="true"` on the right button and the matching
  "You said …" line; tapping the selected answer fires no mutation (AC9); a rejected mutation restores the
  previous selection and shows the failure line (AC10); the card's link target is `/event/{id}` and a click
  on YES inside a `MemoryRouter` leaves the location unchanged (AC17).
- **Integration / RLS** — one test in `tests/rls/`, reusing the S1.4 harness: sign in as a seeded player who
  has been removed from a team but still holds a response row on one of its future events, run the exact
  `useUpcomingEvents` select, and assert that event is absent (AC6). Nothing else here is new; S1.4 already
  covers the read policies.
- **E2E** — Playwright against `vite preview` on the seeded local stack. Sign in as a seeded player; assert
  the card shows the seeded next fixture's title and location; tap YES; reload the page; assert YES is still

  coming up." Third: a bounding-box assertion at 375x667 that both buttons are at least 44px tall and their

- **Manual** — on a real handset, open the installed app after a day away and confirm the card is current

## Open questions

1. Should the card show a countdown or a "starts in 2 hours" line? **Default:** no. It needs a second time
   formatter and a ticking timer, and D35 fixes the formats. The date line is enough.
2. Should a player in two teams get two cards, one per team? **Default:** no. One card, the soonest event,
   with the team name on it (AC3). Two cards would push the buttons below the fold on a 375px screen.
3. Should the card stay on screen once `starts_at` passes while the player is looking at it? **Default:**
   yes, and the buttons disable in place — that transition is [S3.4](S3.4-changing-a-response.md)'s, and the
   next refetch drops the event from the query anyway.
4. What happens when the player has memberships but every team is inactive? **Default:** the "Nothing coming
   up." empty state. Inactive teams stop accepting new events, so the result is naturally empty and no
   special copy is warranted.

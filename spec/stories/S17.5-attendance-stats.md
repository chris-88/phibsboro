## Goal
The Stats tab can show who turns up: per-player games/training attended, attendance %, and availability
response rate — the player their own, a manager the whole squad.

## Context
[X6/X7](../00-decisions-v1.3.0.md). No collection: derives from `attendance`, `event_responses`,
`team_members` and past `events`. See the `attendance_stats` RPC in
[data-model-v1.3.0.md](../data-model-v1.3.0.md).

## Scope
**In**
- `attendance_stats(p_team_id)` — role-aware (manager/admin → all rows; player → own), over past events.
- A `useAttendanceStats(teamId)` hook + an Attendance view (a table/list): name, games attended/total,
  training attended/total, attendance %, response rate. Sortable for a manager; a single card for a player.
- Four states.

**Out**
- The Stats tab shell/routing (S17.7). Performance (S17.6).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **AC1** — A manager gets every squad member's attendance figures for the team; a player gets only their own.
- [ ] **AC2** — Figures are correct against a known fixture (games vs training split, %, response rate).
- [ ] **AC3** — A non-member/anon gets nothing.
- [ ] **AC4** — Four states handled.

## Implementation notes
- `src/api/stats.ts` — `useAttendanceStats`. The RPC does the role branch; the client renders rows.
- Percentages computed in the RPC or the client from the raw counts; guard divide-by-zero (no games → "—").

## Definition of done
- [ ] ACs met; four states; new RLS rows pass on the next on-demand run; `typecheck`/`lint` clean, tests pass.

## The reports
- #2 "on the squad page can't seem to swap from the first team to the second team"
- #3 "Can't drop down PCF II scheduling training/matches"
- #5 "dual managers being able to switch between teams with the dropdown on squad pages and other as needed"

## Goal
A manager (or admin) who manages more than one team can switch between their teams from the **Squad** tab
and the **Schedule** tab (and anywhere the team picker appears), and the whole screen follows.

## Likely area (to confirm while diagnosing)
The manage area resolves its team through `useActiveTeam()` → `useManagedTeams()` and the persisted
`selectedTeamId` (`manageStore`); the picker shows only when `canManageMany` (`teams.length > 1`). The
`ManageHeader` renders the picker. Suspects: `canManageMany` is false for these managers (so no picker
shows), or the picker's `onValueChange` doesn't write `selectedTeamId`, or an effect overwrites the
selection back to the first team. Check whether the reporters actually manage 2+ teams (if each manages
one team, the picker is absent by design — then the "fix" is surfacing the other team, which is a
different question to confirm).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **AC1** — A multi-team manager sees a team picker on Squad and Schedule and can switch teams.
- [ ] **AC2** — Switching updates the whole screen (Selection/Game Stats/Members/Subs, and the Schedule list).
- [ ] **AC3** — The choice persists across navigation (the `manageStore` selection sticks, S6.3 AC7).
- [ ] **AC4** — Covered by a test that would have caught this (a multi-team manager switch).

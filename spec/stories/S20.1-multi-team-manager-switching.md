## Root cause (found)
Not the team-resolution logic — that was correct (verified live: an admin sees both teams and gets
the picker, a single-team manager defaults to their team, a manager of two gets both). The bug was
the **picker control**: `TeamPicker` used a Radix `Select` with the default `position="item-aligned"`,
which does not open reliably in the iOS PWA / WhatsApp in-app browser. So the dropdown rendered but
wouldn't respond, and Chris had to switch team via **Admin → Manage** (a plain button that writes the
store) instead. Fixed by rebuilding `TeamPicker` on `ToggleGroup` — inline team pills, the same
primitive the Squad view switch uses on the same screen, which works there — one tap, no dropdown.

## The reports
- #2 "on the squad page can't seem to swap from the first team to the second team"
- #3 "Can't drop down PCF II scheduling training/matches"
- #5 "dual managers being able to switch between teams with the dropdown on squad pages and other as needed"

## Goal
A manager (or admin) who manages more than one team can switch between their teams from the **Squad** tab
and the **Schedule** tab (and anywhere the team picker appears), and the whole screen follows.

## Likely area (to confirm while diagnosing)
The manage area resolves its team through `useActiveTeam()` → `useManagedTeams()` and the persisted
`selectedTeamId` (`manageStore`); the picker shows only when `canManageMany` (`teams.length > 1`). The
`ManageHeader` renders the picker. Suspects: `canManageMany` is false for these managers (so no picker
shows), or the picker's `onValueChange` doesn't write `selectedTeamId`, or an effect overwrites the
selection back to the first team. Check whether the reporters actually manage 2+ teams (if each manages
one team, the picker is absent by design — then the "fix" is surfacing the other team, which is a
different question to confirm).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **AC1** — A multi-team manager/admin sees a team switcher on Squad and Schedule and can switch teams.
- [x] **AC2** — Switching updates the whole screen (Selection/Game Stats/Members/Subs, and the Schedule list).
- [x] **AC3** — The choice persists across navigation (the `manageStore` selection sticks, S6.3 AC7).
- [x] **AC4** — Covered by tests: the dual-manager `useManagedTeams` case + a `ManageHeader` switch-on-tap.

## Resolved
- Diagnosed "switch does nothing" (picker present but the dropdown wouldn't open on Chris's device),
  not "picker missing". Fixed by replacing the dropdown with a pill switcher. Chris to confirm on his
  phone that Schedule/Squad now switch without the Admin → Manage detour.

## The report
#1g "can we get the Eagle logo at the top centre where the bar is"

## Goal
The club eagle crest sits centred in the top app bar, so every screen feels like the club's.

## Scope
**In** — put the eagle (the crest already used for the icon/splash) centred in the header/top bar on the
nav screens. Keep the screen title if it still fits (title left, crest centre, profile avatar right), or
replace the title with the crest — decide during design at 375–440px so it doesn't crowd the avatar or a
long title. Reuse the existing crest asset; no new art.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **AC1** — The crest shows centred in the top bar on nav screens, crisp on retina, both themes.
- [ ] **AC2** — It doesn't collide with the title or the profile avatar at 375px; deep-link (bare) screens keep their back affordance.
- [ ] **AC3** — Decorative only (aria-hidden or a sensible alt); no layout shift.

## Goal

A manager can see who is actually in their squad and remove someone who has left. An admin can
additionally promote a player to manager, demote one, and correct a mistyped phone number that would
otherwise lock the real owner out forever. The card carries a slot for the reset action
[S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md) mounts one story later.

## Context

The other half of the brief's S6.2, split out by [D20](../00-decisions.md) because it depends on people
existing: members arrive through [S2.1](S2.1-registration.md) and
[S2.4](S2.4-join-by-link.md), which in turn depend on the join links from
[S6.2](S6.2-team-join-links.md). It is story 22 in the build order, immediately before
[S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md).

Decisions this story rests on:

- [D8](../00-decisions.md) — another person's name never comes from `profiles`. It comes from
  `team_member_directory(p_team_id)`, which returns `phone` only to a manager of that team or an admin,
  and null to everyone else. Phone numbers are the account identifier here and are the one thing worth
  protecting.
- [D9](../00-decisions.md) — clients have no INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE on `team_members`. Role changes go
  through `set_member_role` (admin only) and removals through `remove_member` (a manager may remove
  players on teams they manage; an admin may remove anyone).
- [D2](../00-decisions.md) — admin is a club-wide flag and is not grantable through the app. The role
  control offers exactly two values.
- [D51](../00-decisions.md) — an admin, and only an admin, can correct a member's phone number through
  `set_member_phone`. There is no self-service phone change and no profile edit screen in v1 (Q11).
- [D33](../00-decisions.md) — removing a membership deletes the `team_members` row and nothing else. The
  person's responses and attendance stay in the database, stay readable to the manager through the event's
  team, and stay visible to the person under `/history`.
- [D22](../00-decisions.md) — squad is every current member regardless of role, so removing someone drops
  the squad size and the awaiting count immediately.
- [D26](../00-decisions.md) — `joined_at` is set once by the join RPC. A role change does not touch it.
- [D42](../00-decisions.md), [D49](../00-decisions.md), [D34](../00-decisions.md) — cards not tables,
  specified empty states, and `/manage/team/:teamId/members` as a route of record.

Reset links are not this story. [S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md), the next story in the build
order, owns the issuing action, its dialog, the link and the `/#/reset/:token` screen end to end. This
story leaves a row-action slot on each card for it to mount into, so the feature arrives in one piece
rather than half here and half there.

## Scope

**In**

- The member list on `/manage/team/:teamId/members`, below the join-link panel S6.2 put there.
- Name, phone, role and join date per member.
- Remove a member, behind a confirmation that says the history is kept.
- Change a member's role between player and manager. Admin only.
- The row-action slot the reset action mounts into. The action itself, its dialog and
  `useIssueResetToken()` are [S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md)'s, one story later.
- Correct a member's phone number. Admin only.
- Hooks in `src/api/members.ts` and the RPC error mapping shared with S6.2.

**Out**

- Adding a member by hand. "No manual user creation" — people arrive through a join link, full stop.
- Editing anybody's name. Nobody has asked, and the only person who should own a name is its owner
  ([Q11](../00-decisions.md)).
- A club-wide people directory. Membership is the unit; there is no screen listing everyone at the club.
- Granting or revoking admin.
- Deleting an account. Not an app function; the retention rules in `data-model.md` say what happens if
  one is deleted in SQL.
- Transferring a player between squads. Out of scope in the brief. Removing from one and sending the
  other team's join link is the whole mechanism.
- Anything to do with reset links: issuing, the dialog, the link builder, the copy control and the
  `/#/reset/:token` screen are all [S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md). This story leaves the slot,
  and nothing else.
- Showing historic members who have left. They are gone from the list by definition; their rows survive
  where D33 says they survive.

## Acceptance criteria

Each criterion is independently checkable by a person or a test.

- [ ] **AC1** — The member list shows every current member of the team with name, role, join date rendered
      by `formatEventTime(joined_at, 'short')`, and phone number. It is sorted managers first, then
      players, each group by name using `Intl.Collator('en-IE', { sensitivity: 'base' })`.
- [ ] **AC2** — The screen is reachable by a manager of the team and by an admin. A player is redirected
      to `/`. The enforcement is per action, not per screen: `remove_member`, `set_member_role` and
      `set_member_phone` each refuse a player (S1.4).
- [ ] **AC3** — Remove opens a confirmation naming the member and carrying the line "Their past
      availability and attendance are kept." On confirm the row disappears, the squad size on
      [S4.3](S4.3-manager-event-view.md) drops by one, and the person's `event_responses` and `attendance`
      rows still exist.
- [ ] **AC4** — A manager sees no Remove action on another manager's card, and a manager calling
      `remove_member` for a manager raises `not_authorised`. An admin sees it on every card.
- [ ] **AC5** — Change role is rendered only for an admin. The control offers exactly two values, player
      and manager. Admin is not among them anywhere in the rendered DOM.
- [ ] **AC6** — Changing a role updates the badge and the sort position, and leaves `joined_at` byte-identical.
- [ ] **AC7** — Removing or demoting the team's only manager is allowed, and the confirmation carries the
      extra line "This leaves {team} with no manager."
- [ ] **AC8** — Each `MemberCard` renders a row-action slot taking one child. It is empty in this story
      and stays empty until [S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md) mounts the reset action into it. A
      card with an empty slot lays out unchanged.
- [ ] **AC9** — Correct phone is rendered only for an admin. The typed value is normalised with
      `toE164()` before submit; a value `toE164()` cannot parse is rejected inline with "That doesn't look
      like a mobile number." and sends nothing.
- [ ] **AC10** — A phone correction colliding with an existing account shows "That number is already
      registered to someone else." The RPC raises `phone_taken`; no Postgres unique-violation string
      reaches the screen.
- [ ] **AC11** — A successful phone correction updates both `auth.users.phone` and `profiles.phone`, and
      the member can sign in with the new number and not the old one (integration test).
- [ ] **AC12** — A user who is a manager of one team and a player of another renders with the role of
      **this** team on this screen, and the other membership is neither shown nor affected by a change
      here.
- [ ] **AC13** — Phone numbers are never rendered to a non-manager, never written to a log line, and never
      leave in a Sentry event: the scrubber redacts E.164 and Irish mobile patterns from messages,
      breadcrumbs, URLs and request bodies ([D16](../00-decisions.md)), asserted in its unit test.
- [ ] **AC14** — All four UI states are handled per the table below. The empty state is "No one has joined
      this team yet" with the join-link panel above it left visible and usable.
- [ ] **AC15** — One card per member at all widths, no horizontal scroll at 375px, every control at least
      44px.

## Implementation notes

No migration. Every RPC this story calls is specified in `data-model.md` and shipped by S1.3.

### Files

```
src/features/teams/components/MemberList.tsx
src/features/teams/components/MemberCard.tsx
src/features/teams/components/RemoveMemberDialog.tsx

src/features/teams/components/CorrectPhoneDialog.tsx
src/api/members.ts
src/features/teams/schema.ts
```

`CopyLinkField`, `copyText()` and the `mapRpcError` helper come from
[S6.2](S6.2-team-join-links.md). This story adds no URL builder and no `src/lib/urls.ts`: `resetUrl(token)`
lives in `src/lib/paths.ts`, the one file allowed to contain `/#/` ([S0.3](S0.3-routing.md) AC8), and is
consumed by [S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md).

### Schema

```ts
// src/features/teams/schema.ts
export const memberDirectoryRowSchema = z.object({
  user_id:   z.string().uuid(),
  name:      z.string().min(1),
  role:      z.enum(['player', 'manager']),   // asserted equal to Enums<'member_role'>
  joined_at: z.string(),                      // timestamptz, formatted only by formatEventTime
  phone:     z.string().nullable(),           // null unless the caller is a manager of this team or an admin
})
export type MemberDirectoryRow = z.infer<typeof memberDirectoryRowSchema>
```

`phone` being nullable in the type is the point. A component that renders it must handle null rather than
assume the caller's role, so a future reuse of this RPC on a player-facing screen cannot leak a number by
accident. [S4.4](S4.4-player-response-list.md) consumes the same RPC and the same schema.

### Hooks

```ts
// src/api/members.ts
export function useTeamMembers(teamId: string): UseQueryResult<MemberDirectoryRow[]>
export function useRemoveMember(teamId: string): UseMutationResult<void, PostgrestError, { userId: string }>
export function useSetMemberRole(teamId: string): UseMutationResult<void, PostgrestError, { userId: string; role: MemberRole }>
export function useSetMemberPhone(): UseMutationResult<void, PostgrestError, { userId: string; phone: string }>
```

`useTeamMembers` calls `supabase.rpc('team_member_directory', { p_team_id: teamId })` and parses with
`z.array(memberDirectoryRowSchema)`, sorting in the query's `select` option so every consumer shares the
order.

Query keys:

The key is [S1.5](S1.5-typed-data-layer-and-shared-helpers.md)'s, not a new one. Its AC15 forbids a
fourth factory and a literal key, so this story consumes what is already declared:

```ts
// src/api/queryKeys.ts — S1.5's teamKeys, imported here, not redeclared
  members: (teamId: string) => ['teams', teamId, 'members'] as const,
```

Invalidation:

- `useRemoveMember` and `useSetMemberRole` invalidate `teamKeys.members(teamId)` **and** `eventKeys.all`,
  because squad size feeds S4.3's awaiting count ([D22](../00-decisions.md)) and those counts derive from
  the response queries under that prefix.
- `useSetMemberPhone` invalidates only the member list.

Remove and role change are optimistic with the [D48](../00-decisions.md) rollback pattern: `onMutate`
snapshots the list, `onError` restores it and shows an inline line on the card, `onSettled` invalidates.
Correcting a phone is not optimistic; it shows a pending state in its dialog.

### Error mapping

```
'not_authorised' -> "You can't do that."                                  // inline, in the dialog
'phone_taken'    -> "That number is already registered to someone else."  // field-level
default          -> "Couldn't do that. Try again."                        // inline, with Retry
```

### Card

Per [D42](../00-decisions.md), a card, not a table row. Line one: name, with a role `Badge` right-aligned
and an "Only manager" hint when applicable. Line two: phone and "Joined {date}", muted. Line three: a
44px `DropdownMenu` trigger labelled with the member's name for screen readers, holding the actions the
caller is actually permitted — Remove, Change role, Correct phone, plus whatever
[S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md) mounts into the slot. Never render a
disabled action the caller cannot use; omit it. The menu is the only place actions live, so a card with a
single permitted action still uses it and the layout does not shift between roles.

shadcn: `Card`, `Badge`, `DropdownMenu`, `AlertDialog`, `Dialog`, `Select`, `Input`, `Button`, `Skeleton`,
`Alert`, plus `Form` for the phone dialog.

### Gotchas

- `team_member_directory` returns rows to any member of the team, with `phone` null for non-managers.
  The screen guard is what keeps a player out, and it is convenience. Do not confuse the two in a comment
  or in the copy.
- Removing yourself is possible for a manager who is a player on the same team only in the sense that
  the primary key allows one role per user per team: a manager is a `manager` row, so `remove_member`
  refuses a manager to a manager. An admin removing themselves from a team keeps the admin flag, which is
  club-wide. Neither case needs UI; both are covered by a test so nobody discovers them in production.
- Do not call `team_member_directory` from a component render path more than once per screen. One query,
  one key, shared by the list and by every dialog through the cache.
- A phone number is as sensitive as anything this app holds. The D16 scrubber already redacts E.164 and
  Irish mobile patterns; this screen must not defeat it by putting a number in a URL or a breadcrumb.

## Test plan

- **Unit** — Vitest + RTL. Sort puts managers first and is case-insensitive. `memberDirectoryRowSchema`
  accepts a null phone and rejects a `role` of `admin`. Card rendering by caller role: a manager sees no
  Remove on a manager card, no Change role and no Correct phone at all; an admin sees all four. The role
  `Select` contains exactly two options. Remove fires no mutation until confirmed. The last-manager line
  appears only when exactly one manager remains. `toE164` normalisation of `087 123 4567`,
  `+353 87 123 4567` and nonsense, driven through the dialog. Join date renders through `formatEventTime`
  against a fixed UTC instant with `TZ=UTC` ([D53](../00-decisions.md)).
- **Integration / RLS** — extends `tests/rls/members.spec.ts` from S1.4:
  - `team_member_directory` returns phone to a manager of the team and to an admin, null to a player of
    the team, and zero rows to a manager of another team and to the no-team role (D37)
  - manager removes a player: succeeds; the player's `event_responses` and `attendance` rows still exist
    and are still readable by that manager through the event's team (D33), and by the player themselves
  - manager removes a manager: `not_authorised`. Admin removes a manager: succeeds
  - player calling any of the three action RPCs: `not_authorised`
  - `set_member_role` by a manager: `not_authorised`. By an admin: succeeds, `joined_at` unchanged
  - `set_member_phone` by an admin: `auth.users.phone` and `profiles.phone` both updated, sign-in works
    with the new number and fails with the old. By a manager: `not_authorised`. Duplicate: `phone_taken`
  - a direct `select`, `insert`, `update` or `delete` on `team_members` as every role: refused or zero
    rows
- **E2E** — Playwright, in the seeded local stack: an admin opens the members screen, promotes a player to
  manager, removes another player, and the squad count on that team's manager event view drops by one.
  Reset-link redemption end to end belongs to [S2.3](S2.3-password-reset-by-manager.md).
- **Manual** — on a real phone, at 375px: open the members screen, confirm every card's action menu is
  reachable with one thumb and no card scrolls sideways. Recorded in [S7.4](S7.4-device-pass.md).

## Open questions

1. **Does the seed contain a user with different roles on different teams?** AC12 needs one and
   `data-model.md` promises the case is supported. Proposed default: [S1.2](S1.2-profile-provisioning-and-seed.md)'s
   seed makes one of the two managers a `player` on the other team, documented in the seed script. No new
   fixture set, one extra row.
2. **Removing or demoting the last manager of a team.** Proposed default, written into AC7: allowed, with
   an extra line in the confirmation. A hard block strands an admin who needs to swap a manager out, and
   an admin can always manage the team themselves since `is_team_manager()` returns true for them.
3. **Should a manager be able to see a player's phone number at all?** Proposed default: yes, per D8 —
   the manager is the person who has to ring someone who has not answered, and the numbers are already in
   their WhatsApp group. Non-managers get null and the type makes that unavoidable.

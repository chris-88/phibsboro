## The report
#1b "the minutes should be in bold on the player's profile as well because not every player on the team
will get an assist or a goal"

## Goal
A player's own contribution reads even when they have no goals/assists — minutes played is the figure
everyone has, so it should be the emphasised one.

## Scope
**In** — in the player-facing Performance view (Stats tab, S17.6/S17.7) and/or wherever a player sees
their own line, make **minutes** the primary/bold figure (goals/assists secondary). Confirm where the
tester means "the player's profile" — stats currently live on the **Stats tab**, deliberately not the
profile (W8). Either emphasise minutes on the Stats performance row, or add a compact season line to the
profile — decide with Chris.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **AC1** — Minutes is the emphasised figure on the player's own performance view.
- [ ] **AC2** — A player with 0 goals/assists still shows a meaningful line (minutes + appearances).

## Open question
- "the player's profile" — the Stats tab row, or a new line on `/profile`? (W8 kept stats off the profile.)

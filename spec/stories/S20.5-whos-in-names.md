## The report
#1e "you should be able to see what players are going training and players who aren't and who is/was
left to say yes or no"

## Goal
Everyone on the team can see, per event, **who's available, who's unavailable, and who's still to
answer** — not just the counts.

## Context
This is the v1.2.0 Epic 13 "who's-in" (S13.1/S13.2), specced but not built; the tester is asking for it
directly. The manager already sees the full response table (S4.4); this brings a names view to **players**
too. Reuse the existing spec, but confirm the privacy line below — the earlier decision (W3/W4) exposed
**available names only** to players; #1e asks for the unavailable and awaiting lists as well.

## Scope
**In** — on the event detail (and/or Home), a section listing names under **Available / Unavailable /
Awaiting**, from `event_responses` + team members. A security-definer RPC so a player sees names without
reading the raw table.

**Out** — reasons for "unavailable" being shown to other players (the S18.4 reason is manager-facing;
confirm whether players see it).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **AC1** — A team member sees available / unavailable / awaiting names for an event.
- [ ] **AC2** — Scoped by team via RLS/RPC; a non-member sees nothing.
- [ ] **AC3** — Four states; updates when responses change.

## Open question (supersedes W3/W4)
- The earlier call exposed **available names only** to players. #1e wants all three lists. Confirm players
  may see who said **no** (and whether the reason shows) — a small privacy decision for the club.

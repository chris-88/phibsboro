## Goal

## Context

## Scope
**In**
- The Squad tab clearly offers Selection (pick the matchday squad), Game Stats (collect a match's stats), and
  Members. For a manager of >1 team, the existing team selector is reused.
- Game Stats lists the team's matches (recent + upcoming that have kicked off) → tap → the collection screen.

**Out**

## Goal
The disabled **Stats** placeholder becomes a real tab with **Attendance** and **Performance**, shown by role.

## Context
[X6](../00-decisions-v1.3.0.md). Turns the W8 placeholder into a live tab; a player sees their own figures,
a manager/admin the squad's. A team selector (reuse the Squad/Manage one) for a multi-team viewer.

## Scope
**In**
- Enable the Stats nav tab (drop `disabled`); a `/stats` route (guard `authed`, chrome `nav`).
- The screen: an **Attendance / Performance** segmented toggle over the two views (S17.5, S17.6); a team
  picker when the viewer has more than one team (a pure player with one team needs none).
- Role-aware framing (own vs squad) comes from the RPCs; the screen just renders what it gets.
- Four states across both views.

**Out**
- New stat computation (S17.5/S17.6 own it). Drill-into-a-player profile (a later view-others epic).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **AC1** — The Stats tab is selectable and routes to `/stats`; the placeholder is gone.
- [ ] **AC2** — Attendance and Performance are both reachable via the toggle.
- [ ] **AC3** — A player sees their own figures; a manager/admin sees the squad; a multi-team viewer can switch team.
- [ ] **AC4** — Four states on each view; a team with no data shows the right empty.
- [ ] **AC5** — nav/route/allowlist/coverage tests updated (Stats is now a real route).

## Implementation notes
- `src/features/stats/routes/StatsScreen.tsx`; reuse the Attendance/Performance views. `navItemsForRole`
  drops the `disabled` flag and points Stats at `/stats`; `BottomNav` renders it as a normal link again.
- Team picker reuses the Manage/Squad active-team mechanism.

## Definition of done
- [ ] ACs met; four states; nav/route tests green; `typecheck`/`lint` clean, tests pass.
